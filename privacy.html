Vigil Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · LumioLab

Short version: Vigil collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

  • Your clock settings (face, colours, font, display mode, schedule).
  • Any photo you choose for the background or the Image face. It is kept in the app’s local storage on this PC and is never uploaded.
  • The app’s own files, cached so it works offline.

Device features

Vigil reads the battery level to show it on the clock, and asks Windows to keep the screen on while it is open. Neither leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. Vigil does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, usage data or analytics.

Children

Vigil does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
